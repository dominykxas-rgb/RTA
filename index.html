Enter these into one channel's 15-band PEQ with the band EQ flat beforehand. Then measure again: what's left after one pass is usually small enough for a second, gentler pass or manual touch-up. Deep narrow dips that don't move when you shift the mic are cancellations, and EQ can't fill them.

How to measure a channel
  1. Flatten the channel's EQ on the PZ-C7 and set crossovers and levels first.
  2. Mount the mic at head height in the driver's seat, pointing at the roof (90° file).
  3. Play pink noise in one channel at a normal listening level. The input meter should sit well below clipping.
  4. Use Slow or Long averaging, wait 10 seconds, then Capture. Move the mic about 10–15 cm and capture again; 5–8 positions around your head works well.
  5. Tick PEQ on those captures, set the fit range to the speaker's passband, and Calculate.
  6. Copy the bands into that channel, re-measure, and repeat for the next channel.
